What's next

Restoration Planning Phase · Southern coastal belt

Site-specific restoration design workshops

Following the ongoing field-validation trips, the team will convene restoration-planning sessions with local communities to design site-specific replanting strategies for the validated priority zones.

Past

What's happened so far

Completed
12–14 October 2025 · Brasília, Brazil

Youth4Climate Flagship Event 2025

This project was selected under the Youth4Climate Call for Solutions 2025 and recognised on stage in Brasília alongside 50 youth-led climate solutions from 34 countries, in the "Nature for Climate Action" track. Project Lead Nawshin Alam received the project's Certificate of Achievement from Vannia Gava, Italy's Deputy Minister for the Environment and Energy Security.

Completed
October 2025

Youth4Climate Certificate of Achievement awarded

Official recognition of "Integrated Approach for Strategic Mangrove Restoration" as a selected solution under the Youth4Climate 2025 Call for Solutions, Nature for Climate Action track.

Completed
2025 · Bangladesh Maritime University

Project team recognised at BMU

Bangladesh Maritime University, the project's technical-support partner, recognised the team for its selection and participation in the Youth4Climate 2025 Flagship Event.

Completed
2025 · Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh

Cox's Bazar field validation trip

Field diagnosis covering Rajakun Bridge, the wind power plant site, Sabrang Zero Point, and Jahajpura Gorjon Bon — collecting ground-truth data to validate satellite-flagged degraded zones.

Funded by Youth4Climate (UNDP & Government of Italy) · Technical support from Bangladesh Maritime University
Integrated Approach for Strategic Mangrove Restoration

An Aqua Lead project restoring degraded coastal ecosystems in Bangladesh through science, technology, and community partnership.
